### About Dataset
A simulated dataset consists of 14706 records/ claims spans from 2021 to 2025, covering 12 regions and 8 productlines.
## Objective
To build a power bi dashboard which helps management to identify the market points and enable leadership to make informed decisions, strategies regarding establishing an insurance company. 
## What the Dashboard Helps With  
* To identify the year wise claim volume, paid amount and which year contributes maximum to the claim volume and paid amount.
* Determine which claim type leads in each year by claim volume, approval rate and payout ratio.
* Do channels have any influence in the approval of claims?
* Which product line take maximum days to settle?
* Which is the age group making highest number of claims?
* Which is the customer segment that has high chance of getting approval?
* Region wise breakdown of claim volume, rejection rate and pay out ratio
* Product line which is more profitable when comparing annual premium and average paid amount?
* Yearly trend of approval rate and payout ratio?
## Tool Used
* PowerBi
## Insights From the analysis
* Life&Protection, Motor, Helth/PMI and Home/Property show the strongest opportunity while comparing paid value, approval rate and lowest rejection rate.
* Claim volume, paid amount both show an upward trend indicate rising per claim cost not just more incidents.
* London & South East together account for nation 28% of claim volume & paid value, and both sit in high flood risk zone.
* Individual segment carries 89% of claim volume.
* Age 25-54 drive the bulk of claims and paid value.
* Younger policy holders (18-24) show modestly lower approval/higher rejection than middle aged bands.
* Life&Protection, Motor, Home/Property, Health/PMI, Pet always lead in claim volume, payout ratio and approval rate.
* Time affects severity-claim costs are inflating than claim counts across the 5 year window.
* Channel has almost no effect. The consistency is actually a good sign of stable processing quality, not a risk factor.
## Actionable Recommendation
* Claim cost is rising faster than claim count, so premiums need to move with it.
* Lead with Home/Property, Motor, Health/PMI and Life & Protection. They are the most efficient lines (92–97% approval, fast settlement). Reprice Life & Protection, Motor, Home/Property first, because its premium is the thinnest against payout.
* Don’t over-invest in channel strategy. Channels don’t affect outcomes.
